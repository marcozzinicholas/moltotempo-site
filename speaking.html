Speaking

Keynotes, panels, and executive conversations.

Guy speaks the way he advises: plainly, and from experience. Having led a firm as CEO, steered it through a merger, and served on boards from universities to ESOPs, he brings audiences frameworks they can put to work the next morning. No inspiration without application.

Topics

  • Leading Through Change What it actually takes to move an organization through uncertainty: strategy in changing times, disciplined execution, and the candid conversations leaders avoid.
  • Organizational Resilience & Culture Building a culture that holds together under pressure: alignment, trust, and the habits that let teams absorb change without losing momentum.
  • The Intelligent Use of Technology An engineer’s perspective on adopting technology deliberately: where it creates leverage, where it creates noise, and how boards should think about it.
