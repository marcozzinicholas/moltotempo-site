GeoHeroes

Guy’s interview series on the GBA Podcast
Geoprofessional Business Association

Guy hosts GeoHeroes, an interview series on the GBA Podcast — the podcast of the Geoprofessional Business Association, of which Guy is a former president. Each episode brings on a leader of a geoprofessional firm to talk about the career behind the title, the lessons that shaped it, and how they navigated the hard parts.

Every GeoHeroes episode is on this page, newest first, and each one plays right here — no app, no account.
